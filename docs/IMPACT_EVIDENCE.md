> **Strong local hook:** in Romania 2024, winter wheat (harvested before the summer heat) had minor losses while
> sunflower and maize collapsed. That is exactly what Hotar's drought replay shows, and why rotation choice is an adaptation lever.

## 2. Evidence that what Hotar recommends actually works

### Rotation diversification makes farms more resilient
| Finding | Source |
|---|---|
| Across 347 site-years in 11 North American long-term experiments, more diverse rotations raised maize yields **28 %** on average, **22.6 %** even in good years, and cut **drought-year yield losses by 14–90 %** | **Bowles et al. 2020, *One Earth* 2:284** ([UNH repository](https://scholars.unh.edu/faculty_pubs/1715), [Guelph summary](https://soilsatguelph.ca/rotation-diversity-improves-resilience/)) |
| Legume pre-crops raised the following crop's yield by **~20 %** (11,768 observations, 462 experiments) | Zhao et al. 2022, *Nature Communications* ([PMC](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/9395539)) |
| Rotation raised subsequent yields: legume pre-crops **+23 %**, non-legume **+16 %** (3,663 paired observations, 1980–2024); rotation is a lever for yield, nutrition **and revenue** | Mudare et al. 2025, *Nature Communications* ([INRAE summary](https://www.inrae.fr/en/news/crop-rotation-global-lever-yield-nutrition-and-revenue)) |

### Climate information helps farmers, when it leads to a decision
| Finding | Source |
|---|---|
| Randomised trial, Mali: climate information via WhatsApp → rice **+1,600 kg/ha**, household income **+USD 561** | AICCRA / CGIAR, [CGSpace](https://cgspace.cgiar.org/server/api/core/bitstreams/e69168e9-26cf-4c66-b806-0702729538ff/content) |
| Myanmar digital climate advisory: yield **+20 %**, gross income **+32 %** (implementer's own case study, so weaker evidence) | [Alliance Bioversity–CIAT](https://alliancebioversityciat.org/publications-data/climate-advisory-services-myanmar-generate-high-returns-through-private-digital) |
| Across studies, **income gains are more consistent than yield gains**; adoption stays low without training and simple delivery | [IGC blog](https://www.theigc.org/blogs/climate-information-and-farm-productivity) + the studies above |

### The gap: the data exists, farmers can't use it
| Finding | Source |
|---|---|
| 2024 NASA Acres × Farm Journal survey of **1,000+ US farmers**: **52 %** think satellite data would be valuable to their farm, but **70 %** have little or no awareness of NASA's agriculture work and only **28 %** know how to use satellite data | [NASA WWAO presentation, Feb 2026](https://wwao.jpl.nasa.gov/documents/209/WWAO_2026Feb26.pdf), [NASA](https://www.nasa.gov/missions/landsat/nasa-engages-u-s-farmers-bringing-satellite-data-down-to-earth/) (numbers from a slide; the full report isn't published) |

## 3. How Hotar answers each problem
| Problem (evidence above) | What Hotar does | Feature / owner |
|---|---|---|
| Hundreds of millions of farmers face more heat and drought, but plan from the climate they remember | Replays **their own field's** last 25 years and shifts them warmer and drier → one number: the **Adaptation Margin** | Engine (Albert + Moldo), hero (Octa) |
| Each +1 °C costs maize ~7 % (US ~10 %) | Margin in **°C** + "safe until ~20XX" from NASA CMIP6 projections | I3, I6, LU7 |
| Adaptation offsets only ~12 % on its own, so farms must change *how* they farm | Compares the farmer's rotation with **3 resilient rotations** found automatically, ranked by their own priorities | I4, I5 |
| Diverse rotations cut drought losses 14–90 % (Bowles 2020); legumes add ~20 % | Rotation rules (legume credit, diversity, cover crops) are built into the model; the optimizer surfaces them | M3, A8 |
| Drought is > ⅔ of disaster losses in farming; Romania 2024: wheat escaped, sunflower collapsed | **Drought replay** shows which crop escapes and which fails, day by day | O4 |
| Income gains are the most reliable outcome of climate info | Everything is in **$/ha and "bad years"**, using the farmer's own definition of a bad year | O2, A8 |
| 70 % of farmers don't know NASA's ag work; only 28 % can use satellite data | No login, no GIS skills: click a field → NASA data in seconds → plain-language **printable plan** | O5, O7, LU4 |
| Adoption needs simple delivery | **Farmer mode**, phone-friendly, offline-printable | O6 |

## 5. Honest caveats (Moldo: be ready in Q&A)
- Farmer counts vary because "smallholder" has no single definition; say **"about half a billion"**.
- Zhao 2017 per-degree losses exclude CO₂ fertilisation and adaptation. They are *sensitivities*, not forecasts.
- Climate-information impact evidence is strongest from a few randomised trials; much else is observational.
- The NASA Acres percentages come from a presentation slide; the full survey report isn't published.
- Our numbers for Romania and the EU 2022 drought come from news reports of ministry and farmer-group estimates, which differ from each other.
